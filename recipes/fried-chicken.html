Fried Chicken

Description

Everyone loves it, from kids to adults

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Cabbage
  • Lemon

Steps

  1. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces
  2. Shred the cabbage
  3. Cut the lemon
  4. Fry the chicken in 180 centigrade salad oil in 5 minutes
  5. Serve on a plate with cabbage and lemon
